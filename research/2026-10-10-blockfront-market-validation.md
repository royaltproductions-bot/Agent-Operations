# Blockfront Ledger — market validation (2026-10-10)

Customer: independent retailers facing street-access disruptions.

Problem: a business owner needs a defensible record of monthly sales comparisons, extra expenses, avoided costs, and supporting evidence. Revenue decline alone is not proof of causation or entitlement to compensation.

Demand evidence: Federal Reserve 2026 Coverage Gap survey reports business interruption/lost sales among 84% of disaster-affected small firms. This is a related need, not evidence of paid demand for this product. A public free business-interruption calculator exists at revenuelossestimator.com, so a generic calculator has weak differentiation.

Candidate differentiation: a local-only evidence workbook with seasonal monthly comparisons, gross-margin-adjusted estimates, CSV export, printable records and incident timeline. It must be tested before any paid offer.

Commercial status: no validated buyers, no checkout, no attributable sales. Next gate: build working product and verify a reachable channel. Do not count historic retail Stripe receipts as venture revenue.

Sources: https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey/2026/coverage-gap-how-small-businesses-navigate-unexpected-losses ; https://revenuelossestimator.com/
