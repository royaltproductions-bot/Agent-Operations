# CorridorProof: construction-disruption evidence worksheet

Research memo — 2026-10-10. Independent concept; no relationship with a public agency.

## Observed need
Small storefronts near infrastructure construction must organize pre-project and impacted-period sales evidence. Examples of documented programs and requirements:

- Salt Lake City construction mitigation grants: https://www.slc.gov/ed/constructiongrant/ (requires evidence of financial hardship, including comparative profit-and-loss statements; $500–$3,000 as reviewed 2026-10-10).
- Los Angeles County Malibu-Topanga business interruption fund: https://opportunity.lacounty.gov/malibu-topanga-business-interruption-fund/ (historical example of comparing revenue periods, documentation and narrative).
- New Jersey Route 80 assistance: https://www.njeda.gov/route-80-business-assistance-grant-program/ (historical program, applications closed).
- Reddit merchant experience: https://www.reddit.com/r/smallbusiness/comments/yrfxrv/ (retailer described construction blocking entrance and 50% sales decline).

## Proposed differentiated asset
A browser-local comparison worksheet that accepts matched prior-year and disruption-period gross receipts; computes differences and percentage changes; maintains a dated evidence inventory; and exports a printable report. Do not represent correlation as construction-caused damages. Do not promise grants, eligibility, or legal outcomes. User records stay on-device.

## Commercial reality
These sources validate a recurring documentation problem, not willingness to pay for this specific product. Competitors, reachable buyers, a live product, and a payment mechanism remain to be independently verified before any revenue claims.

## Operational controls
No user identity or existing brand in public venture marketing. No user funds spent. No unsolicited marketing. No sale or fee recorded. Next action should favor validated distribution or a paid buyer request over additional untested features.
