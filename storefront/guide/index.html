Questions before ordering?

What's included

One vendor summary and one transaction-level audit CSV from a single anonymized reporting period, up to 500 rows. The service does not transfer money or provide accounting, tax, or legal advice.

What file do I provide?

A CSV with date, vendor, item, transaction_id, gross, refund, fee, and vendor_rate. Up to 500 sales data rows, one period.

What should I omit?

Exclude customers' names, email addresses, account numbers, card numbers, government IDs and other personal records. Use vendor labels and sales figures only.

When will I receive it?

Reports are typically emailed within three business days after a confirmed $49 payment and a usable anonymized CSV have been received.
