*Estimated spend delta = (current normalized unit cost − prior normalized unit cost) × current pack units × packs purchased. Positive means higher spend at the same current quantity. Missing purchase quantities are excluded. Differences are not automatically vendor overcharges or recoverable credits; verify pack specifications, substitutions, discounts, freight, tax and contract terms. Item matching is exact by vendor + SKU (or description when no SKU).

Data handling: files are read only in your browser. Export your report to retain it. This tool does not save, upload, or share your records. Limitations: it does not read PDFs, detect fraud, or reconcile quantities received. It is a decision aid, not an accounting audit or a promise of savings. © 2026 Purveyor Drift utility.
