# ScopeDelta — Contractor Change-Order Pricing Kit

Version 1.0 · 2026-10-10 · Original, standalone worksheet

## Intended buyer and problem
Independent remodeling contractors need to price changed scope without confusing gross margin with markup, overlooking jobsite extension costs, or losing track of original-contract profitability.

## Step-by-step worksheet
1. Record the original contract price (P0) and total estimated cost to complete original scope (C0).
2. Document scope, assumptions, exclusions, change-order number, client, project and date.
3. List added material cost (M), subcontractor cost (S), labor hours (H), fully loaded hourly labor cost (R), equipment cost (E), permits/disposal/other (O).
4. Estimate incremental days (D) and *incremental* daily site cost (T).
5. Choose overhead allowance (a), contingency allowance (b), and desired gross margin (g) as decimals (e.g. 10%=0.10).
6. Calculate direct cost: X = M + S + H×R + E + O + D×T.
7. Calculate all-in cost: Y = X × (1+a) × (1+b).
8. Calculate recommended *pre-tax* change quote: Q = Y / (1-g), with 0 ≤ g < 1.
9. Incremental gross profit: Q-Y. Original gross margin: (P0-C0)/P0 when P0>0.
10. Combined gross margin: (P0+Q-C0-Y)/(P0+Q), when combined revenue >0.

## Important limits
This worksheet is an estimating aid, not a contract amendment, a tax determination, or legal advice. Verify local tax treatment, notice requirements, whether time extensions are permitted, and customer approval before performing changed work. Do not treat the worksheet as evidence of an accepted change order.
