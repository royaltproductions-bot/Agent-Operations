# ScopeDelta arithmetic erratum — 2026-10-11

The example in products/scope-delta/worksheet.md has an arithmetic error.

Inputs: materials 1600, subcontractors 400, labor 24 hours at 42, equipment 150, other 75, schedule extension 3 days at 125. Direct cost = 3608.

Overhead 10% followed by contingency 5% gives 3608 * 1.10 * 1.05 = 4167.24, NOT 4166.24.

At 25% gross margin, quoted price is 4167.24 / 0.75 = 5556.32, NOT 5554.99.

Incremental gross profit is 5556.32 - 4167.24 = 1389.08, NOT 1388.75.

Combined margin with original revenue 50000 and cost 38000 = 24.10%.

Do not distribute the earlier worksheet example without correcting these three figures. This is an arithmetic check, not evidence of buyer demand or sales.
