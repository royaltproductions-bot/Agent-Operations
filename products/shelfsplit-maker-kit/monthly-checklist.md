# Monthly maker consignment reconciliation checklist

[ ] Shop agreement, maker share, fees and payout timing checked against signed terms.
[ ] SKU-level beginning stock agreed to prior month ending stock.
[ ] Deliveries supported by dated packing lists and shop acknowledgments.
[ ] Shop-reported sales quantities and unit prices verified; discounts, tax and fees separately identified.
[ ] Unsold returns and damaged goods supported by return notes; not confused with customer refunds.
[ ] Ending stock = beginning + deliveries − sold − unsold returns; unexplained differences flagged.
[ ] Maker earned = sum of sale-line gross × contractual maker share; rounding policy documented.
[ ] Payouts recorded only after actual receipt; outstanding balance independently checked.
[ ] Original shop statements, receipts, packing lists and correspondence retained securely.
[ ] Statements and ledger backups exported and stored privately.

This checklist supports bookkeeping but is not professional financial, tax or legal advice.
