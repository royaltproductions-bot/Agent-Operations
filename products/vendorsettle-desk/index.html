How the math works

For each line: net = gross − refunds − fees; vendor payout = round(net × vendor rate); store share = net − vendor payout. Vendor-specific rates override the default. Duplicate-looking lines and negative vendor balances are flagged for human review. The tool does not reconcile against deposits, apply tax rules, or authorize payments.

Privacy: all processing happens in your browser. Avoid entering sensitive information in publicly shared files. Results are informational, not accounting, legal, or tax advice.
