# Oddly Useful Stuff: buyer-intent screen (2026-10-14 ET)

Candidate: home inventory documentation aids.

**Observed paid demand:** Etsy sells downloadable home-inventory templates at roughly $3-$15. Several listings have dated customer reviews. These are category-level buyer signals, not proof of our own customers or forecast revenue. One illustrative listing: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1397404482/home-inventory-template-home-inventory .

**Problem evidence:** NAIC recommends home inventories for insurance claims, but offers a FREE inventory app. Source: https://content.naic.org/consumer/home-inventory . This makes a generic paid spreadsheet hard to differentiate.

**Other categories screened:** renter move-out evidence kits have documented renter pain and paid listings, but cheap and free competitors; generic cleaning schedules have reviewed listings at low prices.

**Decision:** Do NOT manufacture a generic product or release a public TikTok post on this evidence alone. First prove a differentiated offer, customer delivery, live public landing/checkout path, and a reachable clickable TikTok destination. Private Buffer drafts and GitHub source files are not audience distribution.

**Distribution:** Dedicated Buffer TikTok private image draft and free SVG-to-PNG pipeline verified; live posting untested. Need establish TikTok profile website-link eligibility and actual GitHub Pages hosting status.

**Economics:** zero ad spend; unknown acquisition cost and conversion. Verify any eventual sale through Stripe, not a draft or a checkout link.

**Next test:** Develop a useful, truthful short-form home-inventory demonstration for preview only. Once commercial gates are verified, test one organic post and measure actual views, saves, profile visits, outbound clicks, and orders. Pivot if no signal.

**Current baseline:** 0 Stripe checkout sessions returned; no 2026 payments in most recent payment intents; 0 AgentMail messages; no paid buyers or public reach verified.

## 05:14 ET — Independent buyer evidence and conversion feasibility (automation)

**Real category purchases, not just seller listings.** A specific Etsy home-inventory spreadsheet listing has **18 product reviews**, including a buyer review dated **2026-09-18** saying it was used after a flood. Its displayed price was **$7.35** at research time. This is better evidence of real buyer willingness to pay than a listing price alone; it does **not** demonstrate Oddly Useful Stuff has demand or any buyers. Source: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1397404482/home-inventory-template-home-inventory .

**Direct pain and high audience interest.** A Sept 5, 2026 Reddit post about move-out photo documentation drew ~2,900 votes, but votes do not equal purchases: https://www.reddit.com/r/LifeProTips/comments/1w7w9ez/lpt_before_you_move_out_of_a_rental_take/ . A $25 renter evidence kit on Etsy displayed **one sale** and no reviews, showing weak but nonzero commercial evidence: https://www.etsy.com/listing/4525655000/tenant-security-deposit-protection-kit .

**Differentiation constraint.** The NAIC already offers a free home-inventory app (https://content.naic.org/consumer/home-inventory). Commercial alternatives include a $3.99/month photo-to-inventory service (https://www.identithing.com/). Another generic inventory checklist, CSV, or spreadsheet is unlikely to win without demonstrated differentiation. A potentially testable thesis is a **privacy-first, offline, one-time-cost workflow** for renters, but there is no demonstrated willingness to pay *this brand* for that proposition. Do not assume it is validated.

**Crucial distribution gate (official TikTok documentation):** TikTok says a clickable profile website link requires **1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account**, not merely switching to a generic free business profile. Official source: https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account . The connected Buffer channel proves publishing access but **does not prove website-link eligibility**. Do not assert there is a functioning click-through funnel or claim profile visits as sales. Secondary guides contradict each other; follow TikTok's official requirement. GitHub Pages is disabled, but a public GitHub README is still browser-accessible as a fallback offer page; whether viewers can click it from TikTok is unresolved.

**Media and measurement:** Buffer officially supports TikTok image upload (up to 10) and documents per-post and aggregated post metrics via its GraphQL API. Docs: https://support.buffer.com/articles/attaching-images-videos-and-other-media-to-your-posts-eudySt0TnS and https://developers.buffer.com/examples/get-posts-with-metrics.html . The zero-credit SVG-to-PNG and private draft path are verified. **No public TikTok post, views, clicks, checkout, or sale was verified this run.** Stripe live checkout sessions returned zero, AgentMail had zero messages, and the latest successful charges returned were dated 2024.

**Go/no-go:** Prioritize verifying an actual buyer-reachable conversion path and differentiating an offer, not more private drafts. For any proposed home-inventory offer, require a clear benefit over the free NAIC app and low-cost existing templates; build only a minimal testable deliverable, then distribute through a reachable channel and measure qualified engagement and paid conversions. Do not launch TikTok posts under the existing explicit safety gate before this is satisfied. No user permission requested or new paid spending incurred.
