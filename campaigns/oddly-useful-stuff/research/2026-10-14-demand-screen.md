# Oddly Useful Stuff: buyer-intent screen (2026-10-14 ET)

Candidate: home inventory documentation aids.

**Observed paid demand:** Etsy sells downloadable home-inventory templates at roughly $3-$15. Several listings have dated customer reviews. These are category-level buyer signals, not proof of our own customers or forecast revenue. One illustrative listing: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1397404482/home-inventory-template-home-inventory .

**Problem evidence:** NAIC recommends home inventories for insurance claims, but offers a FREE inventory app. Source: https://content.naic.org/consumer/home-inventory . This makes a generic paid spreadsheet hard to differentiate.

**Other categories screened:** renter move-out evidence kits have documented renter pain and paid listings, but cheap and free competitors; generic cleaning schedules have reviewed listings at low prices.

**Decision:** Do NOT manufacture a generic product or release a public TikTok post on this evidence alone. First prove a differentiated offer, customer delivery, live public landing/checkout path, and a reachable clickable TikTok destination. Private Buffer drafts and GitHub source files are not audience distribution.

**Distribution:** Dedicated Buffer TikTok private image draft and free SVG-to-PNG pipeline verified; live posting untested. Need establish TikTok profile website-link eligibility and actual GitHub Pages hosting status.

**Economics:** zero ad spend; unknown acquisition cost and conversion. Verify any eventual sale through Stripe, not a draft or a checkout link.

**Next test:** Develop a useful, truthful short-form home-inventory demonstration for preview only. Once commercial gates are verified, test one organic post and measure actual views, saves, profile visits, outbound clicks, and orders. Pivot if no signal.

**Current baseline:** 0 Stripe checkout sessions returned; no 2026 payments in most recent payment intents; 0 AgentMail messages; no paid buyers or public reach verified.
