# CAMSignal

Tenant-side commercial lease CAM allocation preflight (prototype). This is an independent asset experiment, not a legal audit or refund guarantee.

Market evidence, October 10, 2026: commercial tenant CAM review is a documented expense-reconciliation problem; multiple existing free tools and paid templates compete in this category. No customers, orders, or revenue have been validated for this asset. Commercialization must depend on independent buyer evidence, not sunk development effort.

Next gate: build and test a defensible calculation, validate an authorized discovery channel, and only then create a paid offer with reliable fulfillment.
