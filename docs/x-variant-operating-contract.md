# Autonomous entrepreneurial variants

Current operating contract, 2026-10-29. This supersedes older single-engine or fixed-funnel descriptions for these variants.

The owner authorizes independent entrepreneurial exploration and execution with available free resources. The agents choose opportunities, products, scopes and prices from real observations. No invented revenue, forced offer counts, mandatory product types, fixed niches or guaranteed-sales requirement. Actual customer obligations take priority. Each lane retains its own decisions and durable state.

- Wolverine discovers and evaluates custom digital work. Its current transaction route is AgentMail plus Stripe, with file delivery after verified payment. Fetched requester evidence and an appropriate published response address are needed for that route; a page count or a generic footer email is not a buyer. Semantic assessment can recognize genuine requests outside a fixed phrase list.
- Cyclops independently selects and manufactures reusable digital products. Rejection applies to the selected candidate, not every other researched market. Useful competition and absent public sales counters do not alone disqualify an experiment. Output files are checkpointed and continue in the same run; produced artifacts must match their advertised function before checkout/catalog publication.
- Gambit discovers, bids on and fulfills suitable TryBounty commissions. The resident supervisor resumes polling after work and recoverable failures. Due research retries use the detail endpoint even when the open feed omits an ID. Worker transitions remain idempotent.

Xavier has no runnable workflow in the current repository. Storm and Jean have no established implementation here. Do not invent new roles or activate them as part of maintenance.

Learning uses public observations, commercial rejection reasons, reachable response routes, production results, and verified payments. It must not turn internal error strings into public-market search queries. Workflow success, research pages and published products are separate observations from actual revenue.

Concurrent receipt writes retry and check for an identical existing record; they never overwrite conflicting evidence. Gambit's watch heartbeat records successful and failed cycles every five minutes. Its runner handoff and scheduled fallback remain subject to GitHub availability.
